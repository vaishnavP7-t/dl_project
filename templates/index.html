📊 Feature Breakdown — What goes into the model?
PROVIDED BY YOU
ados_pre_comm, interact, play, stereo, scq
ADOS clinical subscores from the pre-test. These are the most predictive features because the label is derived from their sum. The SelectKBest step confirms this.
PROVIDED BY YOU
gender, age_months
Demographic features from the DREAM participant records. Encoded as 0/1 and continuous months respectively.
MEDIAN IMPUTED
eye_rx_mean, eye_ry_std, head_rz_entropy … (300+ features)
Statistical features (mean, std, entropy, velocity) extracted from 600 sessions of eye-gaze time-series data recorded by the NAO robot.
MEDIAN IMPUTED
elbow_left_x_mean, hand_right_y_entropy … (200+ features)
Statistical features extracted from skeleton joint positions tracked over the robot interaction session using motion-capture data.
⚠️ Why does it still predict accurately? The label is ados_pre_total ≥ {{ threshold|int }} where total = comm + interact + play + stereo. So the 4 subscores you enter are components of the label itself — the model essentially reconstructs the total score from its parts. This is a known characteristic of the dataset (explored in our ablation study). The Stacking Ensemble, which combines multiple models, gives a more academically defensible ~98% LOOCV result.
